# Job Description: Member of Technical Staff, Forward Deployed

## Company: Vapi
## Location: San Francisco (Hybrid)
## Compensation: $200K – $280K + Equity

### Role Summary
Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs) are the bridge between sales and our product, ensuring that customers are able to deploy assistants quickly and without friction. You will solve technical issues across the customer journey and act as a champion of Vapi to identify new use cases.

### Key Responsibilities
- **30 Day:** Deploy a large enterprise customer onsite.
- **60 Day:** Discover 2nd and 3rd use-cases within their team.
- **90 Day:** Build product features that make the next similar deployment trivial.

### Requirements
- Experience in a technical role at an early-stage startup.
- Previous experience as an FDE at a top-tier company.
- Bonus: Experience as a startup founder.
- Strong technical problem-solving skills and ability to work in a high-growth environment.
