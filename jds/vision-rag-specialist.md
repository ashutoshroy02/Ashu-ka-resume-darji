# Role: AI/ML Specialist Vision & RAG (Fresher)

## Job Description
We are looking for a passionate fresher to work on Computer Vision and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) solutions. The role involves building AI-driven applications, working with image/video data, and developing intelligent retrieval-based systems.

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Assist in building computer vision models (image classification, detection, OCR, etc.)
- Work on RAG pipelines using LLMs for intelligent search and retrieval
- Perform data preprocessing, annotation, and model training
- Develop and integrate APIs and AI solutions into applications
- Evaluate and improve model performance

**Requirements:**
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science/AI/ML or related field
- Basic knowledge of Python, ML frameworks (TensorFlow/PyTorch)
- Understanding of Computer Vision and NLP concepts
- Exposure to LLMs, embeddings, or vector databases (preferred)
