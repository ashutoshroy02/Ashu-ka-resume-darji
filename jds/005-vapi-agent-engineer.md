# Job Description: Agent Engineer

## Company: Vapi
## Location: San Francisco (Hybrid)

### Role Summary
Vapi is hiring an Agent Engineer to partner with customers and internal teams to design and deploy scalable AI agent architectures. This role sits at the intersection of engineering, product, and customer success, responsible for translating business requirements into technically sound architectures.

### Key Responsibilities
- **30 Day:** Ramp on Vapi’s platform architecture, APIs, and agent capabilities; shadow customer deployments.
- **60 Day:** Lead technical discovery, design solution architectures, and build rapid prototypes with AI-assisted tools.
- **90 Day:** Own end-to-end technical design for enterprise deployments and architect complex integrations (APIs, webhooks, event-driven).

### Requirements
- 5+ years in solutions architecture, pre-sales engineering, or technical consulting.
- Proficiency in agent design and AI-assisted development (e.g., Claude Code, Cowork).
- Strong experience architecting API-first integrations and proficiency in Python or JavaScript.
- Experience with conversational AI, voice/telephony platforms, or LLM-based applications.
